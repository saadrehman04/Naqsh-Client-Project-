# How to add a demo site (copy this block for each one)

Inspiration is NOT a template. The agent must take the idea, never copy layout, text, images or code.
Say how close you want it:
- **MATCH**: recreate this behavior or structure closely, in Naqsh's colors, fonts and content.
- **IDEA**: take the principle only (e.g. "big type over photo"), design it our own way.
- **AVOID**: do not do this.

```
### Site name
- URL:
- Screenshot: reference/inspiration/<file>.png
- Who liked it: client / Saad
- Take (MATCH or IDEA): <section or behavior, e.g. "hero layout, IDEA" / "footer reveal, MATCH">
- Motion to note: <describe in words, e.g. "headline slides up line by line, image slowly zooms out">
- Avoid: <what we do not want from it>
- Use it for: <Naqsh page or section>
```

Example
```
### Fitosauna
- URL: (add)
- Screenshot: reference/hero-inspiration-fitosauna.png
- Who liked it: Saad
- Take: hero layout (IDEA): full-bleed photo, big headline left, outline button with round arrow, small card bottom-right
- Motion to note: soft load-in, slow image settle
- Avoid: bold heavy sans, promo side tab, cart icon
- Use it for: Home hero
```

# Saad's inspiration sites (ALL are IDEA only: do not copy layout, text, images or code)

Note for the agent: these notes come from the pages' structure and content. Add screenshots to `reference/inspiration/` for the visual look.
The sites are e-commerce or SaaS. Naqsh is a quote-based B2B gifting site: take structure and rhythm, never the shop mechanics
(no cart, prices, sale tags, discount bars, currency pickers, mega menus, product grids).

### Fitosauna
- URL: https://fitosauna.com/
- Screenshot: reference/hero-inspiration-fitosauna.png
- Who liked it: Saad
- Take (IDEA): hero (full-bleed media, big headline, one button, small paragraph, small showreel card, "scroll to explore"); numbered feature list ("01 / 07" with a short line each); numbered "why it works" points with images; a comparison table idea; testimonial block with a "verified" tag (only when real); big wordmark in the footer; menu with WhatsApp and a "Get a consultation" button; a loading percentage on first load (optional, keep very short).
- Motion to note: smooth scrolling, soft load-in, image sliders with counters.
- Avoid: cart, product grid, heavy bold sans, promo side tab, medical or health claims.
- Use it for: Home hero, What Sets Us Apart (numbered), footer.
- Naqsh twist: the comparison idea becomes "Naqsh approach vs a fixed catalogue" only if the client confirms the claims.

### Wonder (Shopify interior theme, "Nook" preset)
- URL: https://wonder-theme-interior.myshopify.com/
- Who liked it: Saad
- Take (IDEA): full-width banner sections with a small label above a big title and two buttons; editorial collage titles ("Curated Design for Modern Living", "Organic Textures"); a 4-image grid where each tile has a city-style name plus a short tagline ("Bold silhouettes"); a scrolling word strip (e.g. Craftsmanship · Curated · Premium); an "Atelier" band: a design-service section with 3 short points beside a large image; three tall image cards with a 2-word title and one line ("Pure Detail", "Solid Form", "Soft Light"); short quote cards.
- Motion to note: slow image zooms, hover reveals on cards.
- Avoid: cart, prices, sale banners, free-shipping bar, country picker, mega menu, "Shop the room" product hotspots, TikTok video row, theme-preset switcher.
- Use it for: Portfolio occasion cards (name + one-line tagline), Services page "Gifting Consultations" band (3 points), a quiet word strip under the hero.

### Live Tinted
- URL: https://www.livetinted.com/
- Who liked it: Saad
- Take (IDEA): alternating large promo blocks with a small "NEW"-style label, short headline, one line, one button (use for featured collections, e.g. Ramadan & Eid); a gentle "find your match" quiz idea (could become a short "Find your gifting occasion" helper that leads to the form, later phase); founder or brand story block with a "Meet our founder" link; a press strip (only when real); customizable sets as a menu idea.
- Motion to note: carousel swipe, hover image swap.
- Avoid: dense product info, shade lists, announcement bar, clinical stats, award badges before they are real.
- Use it for: Home featured-collection band, optional gift-finder, About story link.

### Navia
- URL: https://www.thenavia.com/
- Who liked it: Saad
- Take (IDEA): very clear hero (one big promise, two buttons: main action and "book a call"); "Trusted by" logo row (only when real, hidden until then); "4 steps is all it takes" with a small icon and one line each (Our Approach); three ways to work together (do it yourself / done with you / done for you), which could become "how we work with you" levels without prices; FAQ accordion with friendly answers; closing CTA that repeats the promise; soft paper-edge section dividers (try only if they feel premium, not playful).
- Motion to note: simple, light reveals.
- Avoid: pricing plans, app screenshots, playful startup tone, emoji-like icons, lifestyle photo wall, blog cards at launch.
- Use it for: Our Approach steps, FAQ, CTA band, hidden-until-real logo row.

## Patterns repeated across all four (build these)
1. One clear promise in the hero, one main button.
2. Numbered sections or steps (01, 02, 03) with a single short line each.
3. Short taglines under image cards.
4. Social proof blocks that stay hidden until real.
5. FAQ accordion and a closing CTA that repeats the promise.
6. "Consultation" language and WhatsApp always within reach.

## What I could not see
Fonts, exact colors, spacing and animations. For those, add screenshots or short screen recordings with notes on which section you liked.
