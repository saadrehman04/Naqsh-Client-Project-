## Inner pages
- **Services:** short intro, big serif page heading on ivory (no photo hero), two detailed service blocks with image slots, a mini 4-step strip, CTA band.
- **Our Approach:** the 4 steps in detail (Understand, Curate, Customize, Deliver), one short paragraph each, optional "what we need from you" list, CTA band.
- **Portfolio:** client intro text, grid of the 7 collections (image, name, one line). A line states it is occasion-based, not a fixed catalogue. Each collection page: hero image, description, "what's typically included" [CONFIRM], gallery from CMS, **Request a quote for this collection** (prefills the form).
- **FAQs:** accordion grouped by Process, Orders, Customization, Payments, Delivery. Contact CTA at the bottom.
- **Contact:** left: phone / WhatsApp, email, address, hours (only what exists). Right: inquiry form. One dark or terracotta band at most.
- **Policies:** clean long-form reading layout, narrow column, last-updated date.
