## Client's design feedback
- Overall theme: **ivory / off-white** from the palette.
- Terracotta and dark theme should appear **at a few points** for contrast.
- Small touches of the other palette colors are welcome.
- Clean, minimal, premium, modern. Moodboard shows tactile materials: wax seal, leather, ceramic, cork, linen, copper.

## Audience (assumption, confirm)
Business buyers: HR, admin, marketing, procurement, founders and executives. Pakistan first (phone is +92; Ramadan & Eid is a product line).
Copy should speak to someone choosing gifts for their team or clients, with a brand and budget in mind.

## Site goal (assumption, confirm)
Lead generation. Primary action: **Request Consultation**. Secondary: WhatsApp. No e-commerce, no cart, no prices.

## Brand personality
Thoughtful, refined, warm, professional, quietly confident. Plain language. No hype.
Visual keywords: tactile, embossed, handcrafted, calm, natural materials, copper warmth.

## Conflicts resolved
- Contact details: use the client-confirmed phone and Lahore location; email is not provided yet.
- Excel says "detailed catalogue", PDF says "not a fixed catalogue": build the portfolio as occasion-based collections with quote requests; the CMS allows a detailed catalogue later.
- Names differ between files: Excel "Project Workflow" / "Why Us" vs PDF "Our Approach" / "What Sets Us Apart". Site uses the PDF names.
- Two logo marks: the wordmark (NAQSH) is the logo; the calligraphic emblem is the brand emblem (wax seal, favicon, decoration). The "N" monogram on the moodboard was not supplied: do not use it.

## Success criteria
- Looks and feels premium on desktop and mobile; the client says "this is exactly the feel".
- Visitor can find what Naqsh does, see the occasions, and send an inquiry in under a minute.
- Fast, accessible, easy for the client to update (collections, FAQs, testimonials) without a developer.
